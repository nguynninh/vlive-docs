# Quick Start

A quick guide to add the **Interactive SDK** to your app and show your first overlay.

<Callout type="warning">
  Haven't read the [Overview](/itr/getting-started/overview) yet? Check it out first to understand the SDK's overall architecture.
</Callout>

## 1. Pick a platform

Follow the detailed integration guide for your platform:

- **Android** → see [Installation & Integration](/itr/android/installation_integration)
- **iOS** → see [Installation & Integration](/itr/ios/installation_integration)

## 2. Common steps

1. **Add the SDK** to your project (source module or `.aar`/framework file).
2. **Initialize** the overlay manager (e.g. `OverlayManager` on Android) in your video playback screen.
3. **Connect realtime**: call the token initializer (`initToken`) and join a room (`joinRoom`) to receive events from the Interactive server.
4. **Attach the overlay** on top of the player: declare an overlay view over the video area.
5. **Listen to callbacks**: handle states such as `NEED_LOGIN`, socket state changes, or user interaction events.
6. **Test**: run against a staging/Interactive server to confirm the overlay renders correctly and emit-ack tracking works.
